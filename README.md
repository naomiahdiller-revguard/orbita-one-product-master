# Orbita One Product Master

A reusable Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme for testing one-product stores—especially visually satisfying, tactile, sensory, collectible and giftable products.

## Everything is editable in Shopify Customize

- Brand name, logo, palette, corner style and overall visual mode
- Announcement bar and navigation
- Hero headline, copy, buttons, product image and media shape
- Proof bar
- Demonstration video or image, including media ratio and optional intentional crop
- Benefits, visual gallery, how-it-works steps and comparison section
- Native Shopify product, price, variants, quantity, add-to-cart and accelerated checkout
- Product details, shipping/returns, care and safety accordions
- Genuine reviews (disabled by default)
- FAQ, final CTA, cart and policy-ready footer
- Section order and visibility

## Image rule

Product images are never stretched. The default is `object-fit: contain` with intrinsic width and height preserved. Intentional cropping is available only through clearly labeled crop settings for lifestyle media.

## Recommended asset kit per product

1. Transparent or clean hero product image
2. Short H.264 MP4 showing the signature action
3. Clean studio image
4. Texture or macro-detail image
5. Product-in-hand scale image
6. Packaging/what-arrives image
7. Lifestyle or gift image
8. Verified specifications graphic

## Product swap checklist

1. Create or import the Shopify product and variants.
2. Select the product in **Main product and buy box**.
3. Replace the hero and demo media.
4. Rewrite benefits around verified customer outcomes.
5. Add accurate dimensions, materials, shipping, returns, care and safety.
6. Keep reviews hidden until genuine feedback exists.
7. Check desktop and mobile preview.
8. Run `shopify theme check` before publishing.

## Research principles built in

- Product, price, CTA and proof appear early.
- Video demonstrates the product rather than merely decorating the page.
- Shipping, returns and product details are visible close to purchase.
- Mobile gets a sticky purchase bar.
- Sections use vertical accordions rather than easily missed horizontal tabs.
- Performance is protected through responsive images and lazy loading below the fold.
- The theme contains no fake reviews, fake visitors, fake scarcity or automatic countdowns.
